How It Works

Step 1: Create a Tresor

Right-click any folder in Finder and turn it into a Tresor. Your files are now ready for secure sync.

Step 2: Files Encrypt and Sync

Add files—they encrypt locally, then upload and sync across all your devices in real-time.

Step 3: Share Securely

Generate a share link or invite collaborators. Set permissions, passwords, and expiration for total control.

Step 4: Access Anywhere

Open the Tresor on any device. Decrypt only with your credentials. Revoke access instantly if needed.
